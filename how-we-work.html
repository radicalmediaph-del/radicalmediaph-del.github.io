How We Work · 12 Steps

Twelve steps, from first message to launch day

Home / How we work

Most people have been burned by a web project before: money up front, weeks of silence, then something that doesn't look like what they asked for. So here is the whole process, written down, before you commit to anything.

Phase one

Plan

Steps 1–3

  1. Discovery callA free call. What you sell, who buys it, and what this website or system actually has to do for the business.
  2. Scope and quote in writingWhat's included, what it costs, how long it takes — sent to you before any work or payment starts.
  3. Materials and accessI send a short checklist: logo, photos, services, prices. Anything you don't have, I write or source with you.
Phase two

Prepare

Steps 4–6

  1. Site mapWhich pages exist and in what order, agreed before a single screen is designed.
  2. Look and feelColours, type and one homepage direction for you to approve — so there are no surprises later.
  3. Words firstHeadlines and sections written for your buyer, not filler text. The design is built around the message.
Phase three

Build

Steps 7–9

  1. The buildPages built mobile-first, because that's where your customers actually are.
  2. Enquiries that go somewhereForms that land in a spreadsheet you can see, an automatic reply to the customer, and a WhatsApp button for the ones who won't fill in a form.
  3. You review in stagesYou see the work as it goes and say what to change before I move on. No month-long silence.
Phase four

Close & care

Steps 10–12

  1. Technical checksDomain, secure HTTPS, loading speed, phone and tablet layouts, page titles and descriptions for Google.
  2. Launch and handoverIt goes live, and the domain and accounts are in your name. A walkthrough so you can update it yourself.
  3. AftercareI stay reachable on WhatsApp. Small fixes, and an optional monthly care arrangement if you'd rather I handle updates for you.
What you can expect

Three things I hold myself to

You own everything

The domain, the hosting, the accounts, the files. Nothing is held hostage, and you can take it elsewhere at any time.

Nothing hidden

The price is agreed in writing before work starts. If something grows beyond the scope, you hear about it first, not on the invoice.

Straight answers

If a website isn't what your business needs right now, I'll tell you — even when it costs me the project.
