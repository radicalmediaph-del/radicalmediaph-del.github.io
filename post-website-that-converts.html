Five Things Every Business Website Needs

Home / Blog / Five Things Every Business Website Needs

Most business websites don't fail because they look bad. They fail because a visitor can't work out, in five seconds, what is on offer and what to do next.

1. One clear promise above the fold

The first screen has one job: say who you help and what changes for them. Not your company history, not a slideshow. If a stranger can't repeat your offer back after five seconds, rewrite it.

2. A single primary action

Every page should have one thing you most want the visitor to do — book a call, request a quote, download the guide. Secondary links can exist, but they should look secondary. Two equally loud buttons is the same as none.

3. Proof that isn't just adjectives

"Trusted and reliable" is what everyone says. Numbers, named results, before-and-after screenshots and real client words do the work instead. If you're early and don't have those yet, show the process — people will buy a clear method.

4. Speed and mobile, in that order

Most of your traffic is on a phone, often on a patchy connection. Compress the images, drop the fonts you aren't using, and test the site on an actual handset rather than a narrow browser window.

5. A way to follow up

Most visitors are not ready today. A form that only says "contact us" wastes them. Offer something worth an email address — a checklist, a strategy call, a short guide — and have a follow-up sequence ready.

The short version

Clarity, one action, real proof, speed, and a follow-up path. Get those five right on a plain site and you will beat a beautiful one that has none of them.
