Before you ask

The honest answers

Is the itinerary really free?

Yes. You pay when you decide to book the trip, not to see a plan. If you take our itinerary and travel it yourself, that is allowed too.

Do you only do the places in your photos?

No. Those are trips we have actually taken, which is why we use them instead of stock images. We plan anywhere in the Philippines.

Why do you need a deposit?

Because we pay for the rooms up front, per head. ₱1,000 is what turns an enquiry into a confirmed seat — without it the slot stays open to the next person. It is non-refundable but transferable, so a slot you cannot use can go to a friend. Ask as many questions as you like first; nothing is owed until you decide to go.

How do I send the deposit?

Enquire first. We reply with the trip details and the payment account — GCash, Maya or bank transfer. Send a screenshot of the transfer with the name you booked under and we reply with your confirmation. Always check the account name against what we sent you before transferring.

What is not included in a quote?

We spell it out line by line in the itinerary — flights, entrance fees, meals and tips are listed separately so nothing lands as a surprise.

Can you work with my budget?

Usually. And if your budget does not match the trip you are describing, we will say so and suggest what to change rather than quietly cutting corners.

How far ahead should I book?

Holy Week, Christmas and long weekends fill months out. Ordinary weekends are comfortable at three to four weeks.
