Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This policy explains what information Radical Media PH ("we", "I") collects through this website, why it is collected, and what you can do about it.

Information collected

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, company and anything you type into a form on this site.
  • Technical information. Basic, non-identifying data your browser sends, such as device type and the pages you visited, if analytics are enabled.

How it is used

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you asked about.
  • To send information you specifically requested, such as an ebook or a strategy plan.
  • To improve the website and the services offered on it.

Your information is never sold, rented or traded.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers needed to run the business — for example email and hosting providers — and only as far as necessary. It may also be disclosed where required by law.

Retention

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to serve you and to meet record-keeping obligations. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask what information is held about you.
  • Ask for it to be corrected or deleted.
  • Unsubscribe from any email at any time.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies required for it to work and, where enabled, basic analytics. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email radicalmediaph@gmail.com or use the contact page.
