Before you hire me

What does a website cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and whether it has to do more than sit there — take bookings, capture leads, send automatic replies. A one-page site for a small service business is a very different job from a multi-page site with a quote system behind it. You get the price in writing after a short call, before any work starts, and it doesn't move unless you ask for something extra.

How long does it take?

The build is rarely the slow part — waiting on photos, logos and your approval usually is. That's why step three is a checklist of everything I need from you up front. Once I have your materials, you'll see the first pages quickly and review the work in stages rather than waiting for one big reveal.

Do I need to write the content myself?

No. If you have copy, I'll use it. If you don't, I write it from the discovery call — in your words, about your actual services. Nothing is invented: if I can't verify a claim, it doesn't go on your site.

I already have a website. Can you fix it instead of rebuilding?

Often, yes. Plenty of sites don't need replacing — they need the message rewritten, the phone number made tappable, the forms actually connected, and the mobile layout fixed. I'll tell you honestly which of the two you're looking at.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I'm based in Dasmariñas, Cavite and work with Philippine businesses and overseas clients alike — recent work includes a licensed electrical contractor in Metro Manila and an IT support company in the United States. Calls happen over Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp, and I work around your time zone.

Money and ownership

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya or bank transfer for clients in the Philippines. For overseas clients, we agree a method that works for both of us on the call. Payment details are on the payment page.

Who owns the domain and the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the hosting and accounts are yours, and you get the files. Nothing is locked to me, and you can move the whole thing to another developer whenever you like. I think that's the only honest way to do this work.

Are there monthly fees?

Not automatically. A domain renews yearly and some tools have their own subscriptions — those costs are yours and I tell you about them before you commit. Monthly care, where I handle updates and changes for you, is optional and only if you want it.

After it's live

What happens after launch?

You get a walkthrough so you can make small updates yourself, and I stay reachable on WhatsApp. Small fixes are part of finishing the job properly — I don't disappear the day the site goes live.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every page I build gets a proper title, a description, clean headings and a sitemap, which is what Google needs to understand and list it. That gets you found for your own business name and the specific services you offer. Ranking above established competitors for broad, competitive terms is a longer campaign, not a launch-day promise — and anyone who guarantees you a number-one spot is guessing.

Where do enquiries from my website go?

Into a spreadsheet you can open on your phone, with the person's name, email, number and message in separate columns. The customer gets an automatic reply so they're not left wondering, and you can reply properly when you're off the tools.

The other services

What is lead generation, in plain terms?

Getting strangers who need what you sell to raise their hand — a landing page, somewhere for them to leave their details, and follow-up that happens whether or not you remember to send it. A website waits to be found; lead generation goes looking.

What can AI automation actually do for a small business?

The boring, repeated things: drafting replies to enquiries, sorting leads, chasing the ones who went quiet, turning a form into a spreadsheet row and a message. It isn't magic and it doesn't replace you — it stops the jobs you do twenty times a week from eating your evenings.

Can you take over tasks as a virtual assistant?

Yes — admin, outreach, inbox and CRM work are part of what I do. If the work can be systemised, I'd rather build the system and hand it over than bill you for it forever.
